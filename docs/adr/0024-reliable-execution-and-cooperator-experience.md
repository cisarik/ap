# ADR-0024: Reliable Execution and Cooperator Experience

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-09-12

Artifact relationship: **historical** design rationale; retained evidence for AP
maintainers and consumers, discoverable through the ADR index. [AP.md](../../AP.md)
remains the sole semantic owner and [PROMPT_CONTRACTS.md](../../PROMPT_CONTRACTS.md)
owns exact structural spellings. Retain this decision with its implementation
history; retirement requires separate explicit lifecycle authority.

## Context

The Cooperator approved the compact consolidation in the
[initial planning report](https://github.com/cisarik/meta/blob/0d3c9698f54ea450e0b7c189cffd4546642ed788/projects/ap/08/01-ap-reliable-execution/01_report_00.md),
grounded in AP `0cf2cff483a36a4cc2254aa424a7c53bd57a97e9` and the ten D2
field-use findings. The report's historical PARTIAL described native-mode file
delivery, not an unfinished design; later archival preserves that status.
Accepted design does not certify this implementation candidate, its publication,
consumer adoption or logical-whole closure.

Most reproduced failures were task composition or application defects under
existing rules: report destinations without positive write authority, conflicting
action counts and output prohibitions, detached expected values, known unmet
prerequisites, and malformed reports. A correct semantic rule alone did not make
its operational use sufficiently easy. Conversation-only source and compaction
incidents remain attributed evidence, not independently measured telemetry.

The planning review narrowed the discovery: a stop before live work was not an
unambiguous immediate-terminal instruction; accepted cutoff recovery did not
prove watchdog or physical behavior; some product summaries were stale while
others correctly left physical acceptance open. Historical reports retain useful
attributed observations despite structural defects. No product/host repair belongs
to this AP change. ADR-0023's documentary acceptance remains valid within its
recorded limits.

## Decision

Keep the existing architecture and make one coherent documentation change:

- Group the existing task catalog into identity/route, outcome/accepted
  constraints, sources/prerequisites, authority, evidence, and finishing. These
  groups add no fields or mandatory records; task-triggered detail remains
  selective and exact task authority stays self-contained.
- Refine issuance consistency in AP §7: required actions, generated output,
  report preparation and recovery fit one grant's prerequisites, effects and
  counts; known unmet prerequisites are routed rather than assumed complete.
- Bind exact gate expectations in AP §12 to object/property, authoritative
  source and readback. Accepted Cooperator requirements may establish new
  expected behavior; existing implementation is not the only valid source.
- Clarify AP §18's failed-prerequisite, residual-check and finishing boundaries.
  Intended negative tests and already authorized bounded repair loops retain
  their meaning. Reporting and necessary cleanup require their own valid grant
  and gates; no failed prerequisite or terminal expiry supplies authority.
- Place a complete parameterized source-review grant beside the delivery
  contract and a separate blocked-report specimen beside the report header.
  They demonstrate exact output permission, any-existing-report protection,
  readback before notice, correct identity/result and separate Git ownership.
- Consolidate role/lifecycle pointers and show unavailable-source reconciliation,
  useful prerequisite routing, accepted recovery constraints, owner-session
  authentication, and separate implemented/reviewed/installed/physical states.
  Outcome boundaries are examples, not a fixed Worker pipeline.

The three normative refinements are **artifact-detectable**. Their respective
owners name the issued grant and prerequisite/action evidence; gate/source and
readback evidence; and stop/residual/output grants plus execution and saved-output
evidence. No new behavioral-normative obligation or secondary authority register
is introduced. Actual reading, client behavior and independence retain their
existing owners and cannot be proved by field presence.

## Alternatives and Cost

Examples alone would leave stop scope and object-bound expectations less precise;
the accepted design combines examples with the three narrow owner refinements.
A complete protocol rewrite, symbolic DSL or prompt compiler has no demonstrated
benefit sufficient to justify migration and maintenance here. No checker or
prose-mirroring suite is introduced.

Automatic report-writing rights or client-mode transitions would erase the
positive grant/capability boundary. Dedicated copying Workers, mandatory context
sizes, per-commit rotation, passwordless sudo, additional hardware and consumer
host migrations are not defaults. Existing completion routes handle missing
rendering separately from missing persistence without replanning or rewriting
an earlier result.

Complete examples add reference text while replacing repeated operational
inventories. Their cost is maintained example consistency; reduced prompt effort,
fewer execution errors and lower Cooperator effort remain hypotheses until
observed in bounded use.

## Compatibility and Migration

This refines ADR-0023's compact composition and exact finishing guidance; it does
not reopen its accepted roles, initial native Planner, preserved manual/selected
delivery, finite budgets, access profiles or ownership split. Earlier ADR bodies
and historical prompts/reports are unchanged. Apply the refinements prospectively
when an adopted AP revision governs newly issued tasks; candidate text never
governs its own implementation or acceptance.

The standard report header, coordinates, statuses, phase-result enum, session/mode
values and existing task-field spellings remain stable. AP's standard exchange
filenames and activated trace-local mapping remain distinct. No executable `ap`,
project schema/configuration, runtime, managed block, stable distribution tuple,
integration/update command, or consumer pin changes.

Rollback requires a separately authorized coherent documentation reversal;
preserve this rationale and earlier evidence. Consumer adoption or rollback is
separate from AP publication.

## Validation and Remaining Evidence

The approved report's fixed V1–V8 matrix covers successful and blocked finishing,
unavailable exact sources and integration-only evidence, context/prerequisite
routing, conflicting effects/counts and expectations, owner/recovery boundaries,
report/interruption identity, and independent accepted-artifact publication.

Implementation validation uses direct semantic/projection review, instantiated
prompt/report specimens, changed links/anchors and exact diff/Git evidence.
Generated specimens do not mean a Worker executed their instructions. No runtime
or broad suite is required for this documentation candidate. A fresh independent
reviewer accepts the exact candidate separately under the governing baseline and
current grant; the implementer cannot self-certify it.

The accepted harmless finishing rehearsal remains a later, explicitly granted
acceptance activity. Its exact temporary scope and evidence limits are in the
planning report, not a new live AP fixture or authority artifact. One observed
success supports only the route on that client/configuration; documentary review
does not establish practical improvement, physical product behavior or universal
reliability. Publication, adoption and closure remain separately reconciled.
